# Draft an email reply

The dominant request in his history. Get the register right and it needs no editing.

## Reading the thread

A pasted thread ends with his own sign-off `– CT` on its own line. Everything above it is the
thread. Reply to the most recent message unless he says otherwise.

## Output

- The reply **body only**, in chat. No subject line unless asked. No preamble, no commentary
  after it, no artifact, no file. He has twice said he could not see a reply that went into
  an artifact.
- If he asks for a Google Docs version, give the reply plus a separate block that keeps
  its formatting.

## Register

- Warm but direct. Professional, not formal. "Be professional but kurt" is his phrasing.
- **Never use em dashes.** He has asked for this 16 separate times.
- No bullets. No section headings. No "What we found" / "What we did" structure.
- Don't restate the thread back at the reader. Answer it.
- Don't overstate or oversell. Don't pad. Say the thing and stop.
- It must not read as machine-written. Vary sentence length; plain words.

## When facts are missing

Ask him, as a short numbered list in one turn, and wait. Never invent a number, a date, a
price or a commitment, and never leave a bracketed placeholder in the draft.
