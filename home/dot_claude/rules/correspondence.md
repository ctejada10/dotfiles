# Correspondence and prose for other people

Applies when drafting an email, a message, a document for someone else, or any prose a
human other than me will read.

- A pasted thread ends with my sign-off `– CT`. Reply to what is above it.
- Give me the reply body only. No subject line unless I ask, no preamble, no commentary
  after it. In chat, not an artifact and not a file.
- Warm but direct. Professional, not formal. Never bullets, never section headings.
- Don't restate the thread back at the reader. Answer it.
- When the draft needs facts I haven't given you, ask me for them as a short numbered list
  rather than inventing placeholders or guessing.
- If I ask for a version to paste into Google Docs, give me the reply and a separate block
  that keeps its formatting.

For Gmail filters and labels: run the search first and show me real matching results, even a
sample, before proposing a filter. Give me all filters together with their actions in one
response. When you can't tell where something belongs, ask, and offer at least two label options.
